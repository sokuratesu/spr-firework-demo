Add a brand new video

Sony uploads a new video. Here is how it flows to the right pages automatically.

Firework Dashboard What you do
1 Upload the video to Firework
Upload through the dashboard, or we can import it in bulk via the API.
Library > Add Videos to upload new content.
2 Add the correct hashtags
Tag the video with the right hashtags. For example, a new BRAVIA 9 setup guide:
he tv bravia-9 video setup product-overview rsi-extended 2026
Edit Content > Hashtags. Type the tag name and press enter.
3 Save. The video appears automatically.
No code changes. No page updates. The video shows up on every page whose filter matches its hashtags:
Thank You page for BRAVIA 9 registrations
Video Hub under TV, Setup, and All Videos
Product Locker for BRAVIA 9 owners
Sony produces around 2-3 new videos per month. Each one just needs the right hashtags and it flows to all the right pages automatically.

Key takeaways

After launch, you manage all video content from the Firework dashboard. The pages never need code changes.

Each page has a fixed hashtag filter built into the code. You control which videos appear by managing their hashtags in Firework.

To add new content, upload the video and tag it correctly. It flows to all matching pages automatically.

Hashtags can be updated individually or in bulk at any time.
