How New Videos Appear Automatically

When Sony uploads a new video and tags it in Firework, it appears in the Video Hub instantly. No page changes, no developer work needed.

1

Upload to Firework

Sony uploads a new video through the Firework dashboard — a setup guide, tutorial, or product feature.

2

Add Hashtags

Tag it with the right hashtags — for example: pe, headphone, wh-1000xm6, tutorial

3

It Appears Here

The Video Hub automatically shows the new video wherever those hashtags are used. No page edits needed.

Try it yourself below. Click the category tabs to filter videos by product type. Each tab uses a different hashtag filter — the page stays the same, only the content changes.

What happens next month?

Let's say Sony releases a new product — the WH-1000XM7 — and creates a setup video for it.

The content team uploads the video to Firework, tags it with: pe, headphone, wh-1000xm7, video, setup

The next time a customer visits the Video Hub, that video automatically appears in the Headphones section and the Tutorials section. Nobody needs to edit a single page.

⚡

Try it: Switch between categories

Click "Headphones" above — you'll see headphone videos. Click "TV" — the same page shows TV content instead. Click "Tutorials" — only tutorial content across all products.

This is exactly how the Video Hub works. The page is one page. The content changes based on which category the customer selects. When Sony adds new videos with the right tags, they appear in the matching categories automatically.

👤

Personalization: Registered Products

When a customer registers a WH-1000XM5, the system knows their product. The Thank You page and Product Locker can show videos filtered specifically for that model.

The filter would be: pe + headphone + wh-1000xm5 — showing only content relevant to their exact product.

The grid above now shows only WH-1000XM5 content — setup guides, tutorials, and tips specific to that model. This is what a customer who registered a WH-1000XM5 would see.

No new pages needed — ever

The Video Hub is one page powered by hashtag filters. New videos appear automatically when tagged. Categories update themselves. Product personalization works from day one.
